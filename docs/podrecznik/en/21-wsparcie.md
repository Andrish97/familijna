# 21. About the project and support

## 21.1 Familijna is free

Familijna is free and will stay free. Every feature described in this help is
available to everyone at no cost — on a guest account and on a registered account alike.
There are no paid versions and no features reserved for a few.

## 21.2 Buy me a coffee

If you enjoy using Familijna and would like to support its further development, you can buy
the creator a coffee. You will find the cup [Buy me a coffee|coffee] in the footer of the service
pages, next to the [Contact] button (an icon only — the name shows in a tooltip).

1. Click the cup. The "Buy me a coffee" window with a short explanation opens (on a phone, from the bottom).
2. Choose a coffee: **Small coffee** (5 zł), **Medium coffee** (15 zł) or **Large coffee** (25 zł).
3. Click [Buy a coffee|check] (or [Cancel]). The external service `buycoffee.to` opens in a new tab
   with that coffee selected, where you choose the payment method.

> Note: support is voluntary and gives no extra features or benefits in the
> service — it is simply a thank-you. You can send feedback and ideas
> through the contact form (→ 19.6).

## 21.3 Music and fonts

**"Classic" music.** The intro, outro, round transition and final theme are tracks generated
for this app with Suno (suno.com) on a paid plan that allows commercial use. They are not
excerpts from TV shows or anyone else's recordings.

**Fonts.** The typefaces in the logo drawing editor (→ 8.3), on the Host screen (→ 16.3)
and in the "Source" logo are fonts under free licences (SIL Open Font License or Apache),
included with the app. You can use them without fees or asking for permission — also at
commercial events. They look the same on every device.
